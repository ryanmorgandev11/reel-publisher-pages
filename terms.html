Reel Publisher is a personal desktop utility for original videos on channels managed by its owner. It uses YouTube API Services. By using Reel Publisher, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Authorized use

Use the publisher only for channels you are authorized to manage and for content you have the right to upload. Review the privacy policy and agree to it before authorizing or using the publisher. Google handles account sign-in and permission consent.

Publication choices

The owner controls the video, title, description, audience setting, applicable disclosures, visibility and publication time. Queue approval applies to the reviewed video bytes and their selected details. A private test is an upload without a public publication schedule.

YouTube may process, reject, restrict or remove content under its rules. An upload or accepted schedule is not a guarantee of public availability or distribution. The owner can review and manage content in YouTube Studio.

Stopping publication

An unuploaded local queue item can be cancelled in the publisher. Once YouTube has accepted a future public schedule, cancel or change it in YouTube Studio; stopping the local worker or deleting its queue does not cancel that remote schedule. You may revoke authorization in Google Account settings.
