Privacy policy

Prepared 21 November 2026. Owner: Ryan Morgan.

Reel Publisher is a personal desktop tool for YouTube channels managed by its owner. This policy describes the publisher itself and the data it uses through YouTube API Services.

Information accessed and used

  • The selected YouTube channel's ID and name, to bind each connection to the intended channel.
  • Upload identifiers and video processing, visibility and publication status, to resume transfers and verify their outcome.
  • Videos, titles, descriptions, audience and disclosure settings, privacy choices and publication times supplied by the owner.
  • Google OAuth client credentials and refresh tokens, to perform authorized channel actions without asking for a Google password.

The requested Google permissions are YouTube video management (youtube.upload) and YouTube account viewing (youtube.readonly). The implemented publisher uses them to upload videos, verify the selected channel, and check uploaded videos. It does not request Gmail, contacts or Google Drive access.

Storage and sharing

The active client credentials, refresh tokens, saved channel connections and private-test checkpoints are stored on the owner's Windows PC using current-user Windows DPAPI encryption. Downloaded credential JSON files are separate local copies and must also be kept private.

The local publication queue stores video paths, file hashes, supplied text, publication choices and operational upload identifiers. Queue files and resumable-session URLs are private operational data. Original video files stay where the owner stores them.

Videos and their selected details are sent to Google/YouTube to carry out the requested upload or publication. Channel names and non-secret upload results may be shown in the owner's assisted setup session. The publisher does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or model training. This static information site contains no tracking scripts or advertising. It is hosted using GitHub Pages; GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Google's sign-in pages have their own data practices.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Local credentials and queue records remain on the owner's PC while the publisher is in use. The owner controls those files and can remove local connections and operational records when discontinuing use. Before removing a queue, check YouTube Studio for publication schedules: deleting a local file does not cancel a schedule already accepted by YouTube.

You can revoke the publisher's access in Google Account security settings. To request deletion of locally stored publisher data, or ask about privacy practices, contact ryan.morgan.dev11@gmail.com. Revocation and local deletion do not delete videos already stored on YouTube; manage those in YouTube Studio.

Google's policies

The publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
