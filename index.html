A personal tool for publishing original reels.

Reel Publisher helps Ryan Morgan upload reviewed videos to his own YouTube channels and prepare publication times.

What the tool does

The desktop publisher connects a selected channel through Google's sign-in screen, checks the channel identity, and uploads the video and details chosen by the owner. Private test uploads stay private. Scheduled public uploads require a future publication time and approval in the local queue.

This is a personal tool for channels managed by its owner. It does not offer public registration or access to other people's channels.

Your channel stays under your control

Sign-in and permission consent happen at Google. The publisher does not ask for your Google password. You can review uploaded videos and change or cancel their publication in YouTube Studio.

Read the privacy policy and terms of use before authorizing or using the publisher.
